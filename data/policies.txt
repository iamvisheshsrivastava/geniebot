Company Policies and Guidelines

1. DATA PRIVACY POLICY
- User data is processed locally on the server
- No data is shared with third parties
- User can request data deletion at any time
- All communications are encrypted

2. CODE OF CONDUCT
- Be respectful in all interactions
- Provide constructive feedback
- Report bugs and security issues responsibly
- Follow community guidelines

3. CONTENT POLICY
- No hate speech or discrimination
- No explicit or adult content
- No spam or commercial promotion
- Respect intellectual property rights

4. TECHNICAL GUIDELINES
- API calls are rate-limited to 100 requests per minute
- Maximum image size: 10 MB
- Maximum document size: 50 MB
- Supported languages: English

5. INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Critical bugs are fixed within 24 hours
- Security issues are addressed immediately
- Users are notified of any service disruptions
- Incident reports are maintained for compliance

6. SUPPORT
- Technical support available 24/7
- Email support: support@geniebot.ai
- Response time: within 2 hours
