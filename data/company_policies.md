# Company Policies

## Return Policy
- Products can be returned within 30 days of purchase
- Items must be in original condition with all packaging
- A refund will be processed within 5-7 business days
- Shipping costs are non-refundable

## Leave Policy
- Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per year
- Sick leave: Up to 10 days per year as medically required
- Personal leave: Up to 3 days per year with manager approval
- All leave requests must be submitted 5 days in advance

## Work From Home Policy
- Eligible employees can work from home up to 2 days per week
- All WFH days must be coordinated with team leads
- Core hours: 10 AM - 4 PM (timezone appropriate)
- VPN connection with security protocols is mandatory

## Expense Reimbursement
- All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days
- Receipts are required for all claims above $50
- Reimbursements are processed monthly
- Pre-approval is needed for expenses exceeding $500

## Code of Conduct
- Professional and respectful behavior is expected
- Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination
- Confidentiality of company information must be maintained
- Violations should be reported to HR immediately
